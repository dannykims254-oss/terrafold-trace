# TerraFold-Trace
**Predict. Protect. Prove**

AI powered farm to market traceability and intelligence platform for African agrifood supply chains
## About TerraFold Trace
TerraFold Trace is a digital platform designed to improve visibility, traceability, compliance, and decision-making across African agrifood supply chains.The platform aims to connect farmers, aggregators, processors, logistics providers, buyers, and other supply-chain stakeholders through reliable data and intelligent digital tools.
## The Problem
African agrifood supply chains often face challenges including:
- Limited farm-level data
- Poor product traceability
- Fragmented supply-chain information
- Limited visibility of inventory and aggregation
- Difficulties demonstrating compliance
- Limited access to market intelligence
- Post-harvest losses
- Inefficient coordination between farmers and buyers
  ## Our Solution
  TerraFold Trace is being developed as a digital operating platform for agrifood supply chains.
The platform will provide tools for:
- Farmer onboarding
- Digital farmer profiles
- Batch traceability
- Aggregation management
- Inventory management
- Logistics visibility
- Compliance and export readiness
## MVP
The initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will focus on:
1. Farmer onboarding
2. Farmer database
3. Crop and farm information
4. Batch identification
5. Traceability records
6. Aggregation workflows
7. Inventory tracking
8. Basic dashboards
9. Data analytics
10. AI-ready data infrastructure
